<div class="paper-body">
  <p>Establishing a 100,000-person lunar colony requires transporting 100 million metric tons of materials from Earth. This paper develops an optimization framework that compares space-elevator, reusable-rocket, and hybrid strategies, balancing cost, duration, and environmental impact.</p>
  <p>The hybrid model uses Differential Evolution over 1,301 evaluations, then checks robustness with 5,000 Monte Carlo runs. Relative to a rockets-only plan, it saves about USD 12 trillion and cuts CO₂ by 88.3%.</p>
