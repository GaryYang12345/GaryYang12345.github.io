  <p>Voice Turing Test is a WeChat Mini Program game: players listen to multi-speaker conversations and decide whether each speaker is human or AI. I led full-stack development on the native WeChat stack (WXML, WXSS, JavaScript) with Tencent Cloud functions, database, and storage, and designed the UI in Figma.</p>
  <p>The core loop includes audio preloading and caching, real-time scoring, combo rewards, and bilingual Chinese/English support across 7 main pages and 2 subpackages. Gamification—levels, achievements, an invite loop, and a leaderboard—was added to keep players coming back. In the first month the product reached 1,000+ users, 300+ DAU, and 5,000+ valid records. The same platform also supported data collection for a paper published at ICLR 2026.</p>
